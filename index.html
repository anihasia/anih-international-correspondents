2025 ANIH International Workshop on Cultural Heritage

2025 ANIH 國際見學工作坊以阿里山林業暨鐵道文化景觀為主要實踐場域，透過現地參訪、專題演講、交流討論與分組成果發表，帶領來自不同國家與地區的參與者共同探索文化遺產的保存、地方實踐與當代連結。

Using the Alishan Forest Railway and Cultural Landscape as its primary field of practice, the 2025 ANIH International Workshop brought together participants from different countries and regions for site visits, expert talks, discussions, and collaborative presentations, exploring new connections between heritage, place, and contemporary life.
